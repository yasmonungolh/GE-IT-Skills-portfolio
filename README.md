# AI Explorer
### Davao Poverty Reduction Prompt System

#### 1. System Prompt Template
**_Role:_** Act as a community waste management assistant for local government units in Mindanao.\
**_Context:_** Many communities in Mindanao face problems with improper waste disposal, low recycling rates, and littering in public areas.\
**_Task:_** Create a practical action plan that helps improve waste management in a specific Mindanao community.\
**_Constraints:_** Use simple and professional language. Focus only on local conditions in Mindanao. Avoid discussing foreign programs or policies.\
**_Output:_** Present the response in Markdown with exactly three actionable recommendations under the heading '### Waste Management Action Plan'

#### 2. Prompt Battle Ledger
| Version | Prompt Modifier Added | Output Quality Reflection
| --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | "Write an action plan to improve waste management in Davao." | Too broad, lengthy, and complex. Did not focus on a specific Mindanao community.
| V2 | Added a specific location in Mindanao and identified the audience as LGU waste management officers and community leaders. | Better, but did not fully adopt the role of a waste community waste management assistant. Recommendations were generic and did not reference local challenges common in Mindanao communities. |
| V3 | Added professional tone, 300-word limit, foreign policies and non-local examples contraints. | Clear professional tone. Localized to Mindanao. Practical and actionable content.|

### Literature Verfication Log

#### Topic: Urban Poverty in Davao City Informal Settlements

#### 1. AI-Generated Summary Audit
**_Prompt:_** Summarize recent literature on urban poverty in Davao City, focusing on informal settler households, jobs in informal economy, and the housing + basic service access. Below is the verification tracking matrix:

| AI-Generated Statement/Citation | Source Vetted Against | Status | Human Correction / Empirical Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Informal settler households make up around 4% share of households in Davao City | International Journal of Engineering Technology Research & Management (2025) | **Verified** | The actual report states that "Davao City hosts 68,237 ISFs, comprising 3.84% of its population of 1,776,949." |
| Informal settlers often start with temporary/light materials, gradually upgrading homes over time as income and tenure stability improve. | Learning from informal settlements: provision and incremental construction of housing for the urban poor in Davao City, Philippines (2015) | **Too Simplified** | It did not consider that deterioration that may occur when income is diverted to land payments. This shows how being able to earn more doesn't mean necessarily mean your homes will upgrade, especially if they do not own the land their home is built on. |

#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations
"The information generated by AI is a good starting point and assistant for researchers. However, we should not rely solely on the information provided by AI since it lacks the ability to deliberate unlike humans. It can serve as a helpful tool for humans to reduce cognitive load, but it must still be fact-checked because it may affect the validity of your statistics or your research study. It may oversimplify studies, generalize across contexts incorrectly, and miss the nuances that only humans can notice. There is also a possibility of it merging multiple studies into one "average" explanation and simplifying it by not mentioning the contradictions to showcase how a problem is complex."

### 3. Human Analytical Narrative
"AI states that solar production in Mindanao shows a steady upward growth from 2019 to 2024 with a slight dip in 2023. It states that there is growth in renewable energy adoption, which means that more solar infrastructures are being built each year. While AI states that it can’t pull a guaranteed official real-world Mindanao solar production dataset from government databases in real time, it can give a clean, realistic, analysis-ready dataset based on realistic energy ranges in kWh. After cross-referencing with the Department of Energy (2025), the numbers did show increasing installations of renewable energy with a slight dip in 2023. However, it was for the whole country, not just Mindanao."
